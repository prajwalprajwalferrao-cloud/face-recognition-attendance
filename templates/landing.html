How it works

From camera to record
in under a second

01

Register your face

The browser captures 5 face samples and computes an averaged 128-D descriptor. Stored securely as JSON.

02

Open the detect page

Your webcam stream is processed locally every 500ms by SSD MobileNet — no upload needed.

03

Face is matched

Euclidean distance between live descriptor and registered faces below 0.50 = recognised. Label shown instantly.

04

Attendance recorded

A snapshot is saved, attendance is marked for today, and the teacher's dashboard updates automatically.
